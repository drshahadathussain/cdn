## 👋 About Me
Welcome to the centralized Content Delivery Network (CDN) for **Shahadat Hussain**. 

I am a **Mechanical Engineer** and **Materials Scientist** specializing in Additive Manufacturing (3D/4D Printing), Metamaterials, Triply Periodic Minimal Surface (TPMS) lattices, and Shape Memory Alloys (NiTi, Cu-Al-Ni). Currently, I contribute to advanced materials research with a focus on Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF) and microstructural characterization.

This repository serves as the backend for my personal media assets, ensuring fast, reliable, and version-controlled access to my professional documents, profile images, and research media across my digital platforms.
